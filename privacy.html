JobMon Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

About JobMon

JobMon is a private, personal-use job-search management application. It is designed to help organize job opportunities, applications, communications, and related career-search activity.

Google and Gmail Data

JobMon may access Gmail data after the user explicitly authorizes access through Google OAuth. Gmail access is used to identify and organize communications relevant to the user's job search, such as recruiter messages, application updates, interview requests, and related correspondence.

JobMon uses read-only Gmail access and does not use Gmail authorization to send email on the user's behalf.

Data Use

Data accessed by JobMon is used only to provide the application's job-search management functionality.

JobMon does not sell personal information or Gmail data and does not use this information for advertising.

Data Storage

JobMon stores application data in systems controlled by the application owner. Access to the application and its private data is restricted.

Third Parties

JobMon does not sell or provide Google user data to advertisers or data brokers.

Revoking Access

Google account access granted to JobMon can be revoked at any time through the user's Google Account security settings.

Data Deletion

Because JobMon is currently a personal-use application, requests regarding stored data can be directed to the contact email below.
